Subject: Atlas the titan, heavily muscled and bearded, bare-headed.
Pose / action: down on one knee on a rocky crag, straining, back twisted, both arms raised behind his head to bear an enormous armillary sphere on his shoulders.
Details: the armillary sphere is made of broad engraved rings with degree marks, zodiac band and a small globe at its center, larger than his whole body; drapery knotted at his waist whips in the wind; veins and tendons in high relief.
Composition: figure in the lower half, the sphere filling the upper half and slightly cropped at the top edge.
Background: billowing engraved clouds around the crag, sky in dense fine horizontal line hatching.
Feet: plain bare feet and bare ankles with nothing attached to them; no helmet with wings.
Anatomy: correct human anatomy, exactly two arms, two hands with five fingers each, two legs and two feet.
