    02 — Anatomy of an agent

    Six arms.
    One mind.

    Every agent we ship is built from the same six capabilities, tuned to the job. Hover one to find its ring.

    1. I

      Perceive

      Reads email, documents, screens, calls and databases — any input your team already touches.

    2. II

      Reason

      Plans multi-step work with frontier models, picked per task by evals, not by habit.

    3. III

      Remember

      Keeps long-term memory of your customers, policies and past decisions. No re-explaining.

    4. IV

      Use tools

      Calls your APIs, CRM, ERP, browser and spreadsheets — with permissions scoped to the job.

    5. V

      Act

      Sends, books, files and ships. Sensitive steps wait for a human to say yes.

    6. VI

      Report

      Logs and explains every action. Digests, dashboards and a full audit trail.

          05 — Frequently asked

          Questions,
          answered plainly.

          What kinds of agents can you build?

          If a person does it on a computer using judgment and rules, we can probably build an agent for it — research, support, sales, operations, finance, code, voice. If it shouldn't be automated, we'll tell you that too.

          Which AI models do you use?

          Whichever wins the evals for your task: Claude, GPT, Gemini, or open-weight models you host yourself. We're not married to any lab, and we can switch when a better one ships.

          Where does it run, and is our data safe?

          In your cloud, in ours, or on-premise. Agents get least-privilege access, every action is logged, and anything sensitive waits for a human to approve it.

          How long until we have something working?

          A working prototype usually lands in days and a production agent in weeks. You get a real estimate after the survey, not before.

          What happens after launch?

          We monitor it, tune prompts and tools, and ship improvements on a steady cadence. You get a digest of what your agents did and what we changed.
